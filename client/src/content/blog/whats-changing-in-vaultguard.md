Recent updates across the VaultGuard web dashboard, browser extension, and native Android client focus on faster credential lookup, reliable autofill matching, and cleaner account controls.

## Highlights across supported clients

- **Web dashboard & public site separation:** The VaultGuard marketing site and journal now live at `/` and `/blog` with pre-rendered static HTML, while the authenticated vault workspace runs cleanly at `/dashboard`.
- **Site-matching extension flows:** Improved registrable-domain matching in the browser extension helps surface the exact credentials for active tabs while avoiding unrelated subdomains.
- **Android autofill & biometric unlock:** Native Android support continues refining offline-friendly vault caching and hardware-backed biometric unlock prompts.

## Import, export, and vault hygiene

Inside **Settings**, you can import existing credentials or export encrypted/structured backups whenever you want to audit or migrate your data. Check back in this section as new releases roll out.
