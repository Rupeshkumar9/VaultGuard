A sustainable password routine does not require memorizing dozens of complex strings. It starts with one strong, unique master password and a clear structure for everything else.

## Start with one strong anchor

Your VaultGuard master password is the only secret you should have to remember. Choose a long passphrase made of unrelated words rather than short substitutions, and never reuse it on any website or service.

- Aim for at least four or five random words or 16+ characters.
- Store an emergency backup in a safe physical location you control.
- Let VaultGuard derive encryption keys locally so your master password never leaves your device.

## Prioritize high-impact accounts first

You do not need to update every account in one sitting. Focus on the accounts that control recovery for everything else:

1. **Primary email accounts** — password resets for nearly every service land here.
2. **Banking and payment providers** — financial accounts require isolated, generated credentials.
3. **Developer and cloud platforms** — GitHub, hosting consoles, and domain registrars.
4. **Identity and social accounts** — accounts connected to OAuth sign-ins.

## Make daily use effortless

Security habits stick when they stay out of your way. Group entries by category, star your most frequently used logins as favorites, and use the browser extension or Android autofill integration so strong passwords feel just as fast as weak ones.

> Good security is not about willpower—it is about making the safe choice the easiest default.
